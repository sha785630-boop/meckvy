2. Scroll to the live widget + inbox preview

## What Meckvy does
Website widget → one Meckvy inbox for Maldivian guesthouses.
Translation, templates, automations, Stripe pricing.
WhatsApp / Resend email APIs are optional later (not required to sell).

## How a customer goes live
1. Paste the website widget (from Settings) on their site
2. Guest inquiries land in Inbox
3. Reply from Inbox (demo mode works; add Resend for real email)
4. Add Stripe when they pay; WhatsApp later if Meta signup works
